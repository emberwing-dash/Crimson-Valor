Crimson Valor is an open-world RPG built for Virtual Reality, combining immersive combat, exploration, and narrative-driven gameplay.

The story follows a young warrior whose village is brutally massacred by the Demon King. Consumed by grief and an unrelenting thirst for revenge, the warrior sets out on a perilous journey to hunt down his nemesis.

His path leads him deep into the Crimson Woods, where he finally confronts the Demon King. The battle ends in crushing defeat. Rather than delivering the final blow, the Demon King spares the warrior’s life and vanishes—his reasons unknown, leaving behind mystery, humiliation, and a burning resolve that shapes the warrior’s destiny.

## General instructions:

1. Open "Crimson Valor" folder of this repo in Unity 6 Editor
2. Ensure required packages (XR Interaction Toolkit, Input System, etc.) are installed.
3. Configure VR device settings according to your headset.
4. Play the main scene to start game located at "Assets\Scenes\0_MainScene\0_Starting\MainMenu.unity"
